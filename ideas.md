## Selected Approach: Modern Clinical Zen

I will proceed with the **Modern Clinical Zen** approach. This style best balances the "Restore" brand identity (which is scientific and medical) with the user's request for "local roots" and a focus on "wellness and longevity." It allows us to be professional and authoritative (crucial for medical services like IVs and Cryo) while remaining welcoming and accessible to the Columbus community.

**Implementation Plan:**
- **Colors**: Deep Navy (#0F172A), Sage Green (#10B981 - adjusted for web), Teal Accent (#06B6D4), White/Light Grey backgrounds.
- **Fonts**: Montserrat (Headings), Lato (Body).
- **Key Feature**: A "Columbus Wellness Blog" section that integrates seamlessly with the service offerings, boosting local SEO.
- **Visuals**: High-quality service images mixed with local Columbus context where possible (or at least a "local" vibe in the copy).
